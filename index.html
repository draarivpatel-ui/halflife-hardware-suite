    WEEK 04 / DISPLAYS

    GlyphMatrix-128 — Dual SSD1306 OLED + 74HC595 8x8 LED Desk Telemetry Badge

    HW I2CPAGE BUFFER5x7 FONTBOM $18.37

    Two 128x64 SSD1306 panels rendered from real 1 KiB page buffers (8 pages x 128 bytes, LSB is the top pixel row). A separate 8x8 framebuffer is scanned one row at a time through a 74HC595 shift register model, with ghost suppression during the latch pulse.
